Our capabilities

Integrated economic and policy advisory

Modular services that can be commissioned independently or combined into an end-to-end programme.

01

Macroeconomic & Quantitative Analysis

Growth, inflation, employment and investment analysis; economic forecasting; scenario modelling; econometrics; dashboards and data visualisation.

02

Public Finance Solutions

Fiscal diagnostics, revenue and expenditure analysis, debt sustainability, medium-term fiscal frameworks, public investment and state-finance advisory.

03

Trade & Export-Import Strategy

Product-market prioritisation, trade competitiveness, FTA readiness, market-access strategy, value-chain analysis and district or state export planning.

04

Market & Sector Research

Market sizing, demand assessment, competitor benchmarking, entry strategy, customer research, investment opportunity mapping and commercial due diligence.

05

Policy Formulation & Regulatory Advisory

Evidence-based policy design, regulatory impact assessment, stakeholder consultation, incentive frameworks and implementation roadmaps.

06

AI & Decision Intelligence

AI use-case identification, economic-data automation, forecasting tools, knowledge systems, policy dashboards and responsible AI adoption roadmaps.

07

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning

Results frameworks, KPIs, theory of change, programme monitoring systems, process evaluations and learning agendas.

08

Impact Assessment

Socio-economic impact studies, cost-benefit analysis, counterfactual evaluation, SROI, distributional analysis and sustainability outcomes.

09

Investment & Competitiveness Strategy

Investment promotion, industrial and cluster strategy, location assessment, incentive benchmarking and sector competitiveness programmes.

10

Programme Management & Implementation

PMU design, programme governance, operating models, implementation tracking, risk management and institutional capacity building.
