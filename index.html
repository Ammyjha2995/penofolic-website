Solution 01

Macroeconomic & Quantitative Analysis

Forecasts, scenarios and econometric evidence for confident decisions.

The challenge

Leadership must plan amid shifting growth, inflation, interest rates and external shocks, often with fragmented data and little time to interpret it.

What we do

  • Track and interpret GDP, inflation, employment, credit and investment trends
  • Build forecasting and nowcasting models for national, state or sector variables
  • Run scenario and stress analysis for policy, rate and commodity shocks
  • Apply econometrics to test what is actually driving outcomes
  • Convert results into dashboards and briefing notes for decision-makers

What you receive

  • Macro outlook note with base, upside and downside scenarios
  • Forecast model with documented assumptions
  • Interactive indicator dashboard
  • Board- or cabinet-ready briefing deck

Questions we help you answer

What growth and inflation path should our plan assume?
How exposed are we to a rate, oil or currency shock?
Which indicators give the earliest warning of a slowdown?
Best suited forMinistries and state departments, banks and NBFCs, corporate strategy teams, investors

Solution 02

Public Finance Solutions

Fiscal diagnostics and frameworks that create room for development priorities.

The challenge

Governments must fund development priorities while keeping deficits and debt on a sustainable path, and revenue, spending and borrowing decisions are tightly linked.

What we do

  • Diagnose revenue, expenditure and deficit trends against peers
  • Analyse own-tax and non-tax revenue potential and buoyancy
  • Assess debt sustainability and contingent liabilities
  • Design medium-term fiscal frameworks and expenditure ceilings
  • Appraise and prioritise public investment pipelines

What you receive

  • State or entity fiscal diagnostic report
  • Debt sustainability analysis with fiscal rules options
  • Medium-term fiscal framework model
  • Revenue mobilisation and expenditure rationalisation roadmap

Questions we help you answer

How much fiscal space do we really have over the next five years?
Which revenue streams are under-performing their potential?
Is our debt path sustainable under stress?
Best suited forState finance departments, urban local bodies, public sector undertakings, development partners
Discuss this solution
Solution 03

Trade & Export-Import Strategy

Turn export potential into market access and measurable export growth.

The challenge

Export potential is often spread thinly across products and markets, while tariffs, standards and logistics decide who actually wins orders.

What we do

  • Map product–market opportunities using trade data and revealed comparative advantage
  • Assess tariff, non-tariff, SPS and TBT barriers in target markets
  • Evaluate FTA utilisation and readiness for new agreements
  • Analyse value chains, logistics and competitiveness gaps
  • Prepare district, state and sector export action plans with targets

What you receive

  • Prioritised product–market matrix
  • Market-access and regulatory barrier briefs
  • District or state export strategy with KPIs
  • FTA opportunity and utilisation assessment

Questions we help you answer

Which products should we push into which markets first?
What is blocking exporters from using existing FTAs?
Where are we losing competitiveness in the value chain?
Best suited forCommerce departments, export promotion councils, industry associations, exporters and MSME clusters
Discuss this solution
Solution 04

Market & Sector Research

Size the opportunity, understand the customer and choose where to play.

The challenge

Entry, expansion and investment decisions fail when market size, demand drivers and competitors are guessed rather than measured.

What we do

  • Estimate market size and growth with top-down and bottom-up methods
  • Profile demand drivers, customer segments and buying behaviour
  • Benchmark competitors on price, product, reach and capability
  • Design and run primary surveys and stakeholder interviews
  • Build entry strategies and investment opportunity maps

What you receive

  • Market sizing model and sector report
  • Competitor benchmarking matrix
  • Customer research findings
  • Go-to-market or entry recommendation

Questions we help you answer

How big is the addressable market, and how fast is it growing?
Who are our real competitors and where are they weak?
Which segment or geography should we enter first?
Best suited forCorporates, investors, start-ups, banks assessing sector exposure, public agencies

Solution 05

Policy Formulation & Regulatory Advisory

Evidence-based policy design, from first draft to implementation roadmap.

The challenge

Well-intended policies underperform when the problem is poorly defined, incentives are misaligned or implementation capacity is overlooked.

What we do

  • Define the policy problem with data and comparative evidence
  • Review national and international best practice
  • Design policy options, incentive frameworks and eligibility rules
  • Conduct regulatory impact assessments and stakeholder consultations
  • Draft policy documents, guidelines and implementation roadmaps

What you receive

  • Policy diagnostic and options paper
  • Draft policy, scheme guidelines or SOPs
  • Regulatory impact assessment
  • Implementation roadmap with roles and milestones

Questions we help you answer

What policy design will actually change behaviour on the ground?
What will the regulation cost, and who bears it?
How do comparable states or countries handle this?
Best suited forCentral and state ministries, regulators, missions and statutory bodies

Questions we help you answer

Where will AI save the most analyst time in our organisation?

Solution 08

Impact Assessment

Rigorous evidence of what changed, for whom, and at what cost.

The challenge

Funders and finance departments need credible proof of outcomes, not anecdotes, before they renew or scale a programme.

What we do

  • Design baseline, midline and endline studies
  • Apply counterfactual methods such as difference-in-differences and matching
  • Conduct cost-benefit and cost-effectiveness analysis
  • Estimate social return on investment (SROI)
  • Analyse distributional effects by income, gender and region

What you receive

  • Impact evaluation design and survey instruments
  • Impact assessment report with quantified outcomes
  • Cost-benefit or SROI model
  • Policy brief on scaling and redesign

Questions we help you answer

Did the programme cause the change, or would it have happened anyway?
What is the return on every rupee spent?
Who benefited most, and who was left out?
Best suited forMinistries and departments, finance and planning departments, CSR foundations, multilateral agencies

Attract investment and build globally competitive sectors and clusters.

  • Conduct location and site suitability assessments
  • Design industrial, cluster and investment promotion strategies

  • Incentive benchmarking study
  • Investment promotion strategy and target-investor list
  • Cluster development action plan

Questions we help you answer

Which sectors give us a real competitive edge?
How do our incentives compare with competing states?
What do investors need before they commit?

Solution 10

Programme Management & Implementation

PMU support that turns strategy into on-time, on-budget delivery.

The challenge

What we do

  • Set up operating models, workflows and SOPs
  • Track implementation through milestone and fund-flow monitoring
  • Identify risks early and manage escalation
  • Build institutional capacity through training and handholding

What you receive

  • PMU structure, roles and terms of reference
  • Programme plan and progress tracker
  • Periodic progress and review reports
  • Risk register and capacity-building plan

Questions we help you answer

Who owns which decision, and how fast can it be made?
Where is implementation slipping, and why?
How do we build capacity so delivery continues after us?
